Settings

Sync across devices

Sign in with the same username and password on your laptop and phone to keep one copy of your planner everywhere. Everything is encrypted in your browser before it's uploaded, so the server can't read it. There's no password reset: if you forget it, the synced copy can't be opened (each device keeps its own copy).

Outlook calendar

In Outlook on the web: Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar, pick your calendar and “Can view all details”, click Publish, then copy the ICS link and paste it here. Events appear read-only in your slots and refresh every 15 minutes.

Slot-end notifications

When a slot ends you get a notification (with a chime) and can rate it right there: ✅ Completed or Not completed…, which then asks 🔴 Distracted / 🟡 Not efficient. Keep the planner open in a tab (it can be in the background) and click anywhere in it once after opening it, so the browser allows sound. Only slots within your shown hours are included. These settings apply to this device only.

Event categories & colours

Outlook events get the colour of the first category whose keywords appear in the title (matched at the start of a word, so “run” matches “Running” but not “brunch”). If a title matches several categories, the higher one wins. Use ↑ to reorder.

Backup

Your data is stored in this browser only. Export a backup now and then, or to move to another device.
