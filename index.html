Outlook calendar

In Outlook on the web: Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar, pick your calendar and “Can view all details”, click Publish, then copy the ICS link and paste it here. Events appear read-only in your slots and refresh every 15 minutes.

Event categories & colours

Outlook events get the colour of the first category whose keywords appear in the title (matched at the start of a word, so “run” matches “Running” but not “brunch”). If a title matches several categories, the higher one wins. Use ↑ to reorder.
